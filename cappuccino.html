Cappuccino

Description:

An espresso-based coffee drink with equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and foamed milk. It has a distinct layered appearance and a stronger coffee taste than a latte.

How to Make It:

  1. Prepare one or two shots of espresso into a cappuccino cup.
  2. Steam 5-6 ounces (150-180ml) of cold milk until it reaches 140-150°F (60-65°C), creating more foam than for a latte.
  3. Gently swirl the steamed milk and tap the pitcher to remove large bubbles.
  4. Pour the steamed milk into the espresso, aiming for a roughly equal ratio of espresso, liquid milk, and foam on top.
  5. Serve immediately.
