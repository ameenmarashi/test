Macchiato

Description:

An espresso 'marked' or 'stained' with a small amount of foamed milk. It's a stronger coffee flavor than a cappuccino or latte, highlighting the espresso.

How to Make It:

  1. Prepare a single or double shot of espresso into a demitasse cup.
  2. Steam a small amount of milk (about 2 ounces / 60ml) to create a thick, airy foam.
  3. Spoon a dollop of the foamed milk directly onto the center of the espresso.
  4. Serve immediately.
