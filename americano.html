Americano

Description:

A coffee drink prepared by diluting an espresso with hot water, giving it a similar strength to, but different flavor from, brewed coffee. It's lighter than a traditional espresso.

How to Make It:

  1. Heat water to near boiling (around 200°F / 93°C).
  2. Prepare one or two shots of espresso into a mug.
  3. Pour 4-6 ounces (120-180ml) of hot water into the mug, on top of the espresso.
  4. Serve hot, optionally with milk or sugar.
