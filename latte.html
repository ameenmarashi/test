Latte

Description:

A popular coffee drink made with espresso and a generous amount of steamed milk, topped with a thin layer of microfoam. Known for its creamy texture.

How to Make It:

  1. Prepare one or two shots of espresso into a mug.
  2. Steam 6-8 ounces (180-240ml) of cold milk until it reaches 140-150°F (60-65°C), creating smooth microfoam.
  3. Gently swirl the steamed milk to integrate the foam.
  4. Pour the steamed milk into the espresso, initially tilting the cup to combine, then straightening to create latte art if desired.
  5. Serve warm.
