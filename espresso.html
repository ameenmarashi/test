Espresso

Description:

A concentrated coffee beverage brewed by forcing hot water under high pressure through finely ground coffee beans, forming a rich, dark crema on top.

How to Make It:

  1. Grind 7-9 grams of fresh coffee beans very finely (like powdered sugar).
  2. Tamp the grounds evenly and firmly into the portafilter.
  3. Insert the portafilter into the espresso machine's group head.
  4. Start the shot, aiming for 25-30 seconds to extract 1-2 ounces (30-60ml) of liquid.
  5. Serve immediately in a demitasse cup.
