Mocha

Description:

A chocolate-flavored variant of a latte, made with espresso, steamed milk, and chocolate syrup or powder.

How to Make It:

  1. Add 1-2 tablespoons of chocolate syrup (or cocoa powder) to the bottom of a mug.
  2. Prepare one or two shots of espresso directly into the mug, mixing with the chocolate.
  3. Steam 6-8 ounces (180-240ml) of cold milk until it reaches 140-150°F (60-65°C), creating smooth microfoam.
  4. Pour the steamed milk into the chocolate-espresso mixture.
  5. Optionally top with whipped cream, chocolate shavings, or a drizzle of chocolate syrup.
